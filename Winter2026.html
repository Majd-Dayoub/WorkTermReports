Abstract/Introduction

In this blog post, I will be going over my fourth co-op work term as a Cloud Engineering Co-op at Co-operators in the Guelph office.

For my fourth co-op work term, I joined Co-operators, a Canadian insurance and financial services organization that supports clients across the country through insurance, investment, and advisory services.

At Co-operators, I worked from the Guelph office as a Cloud Engineering Co-op. My work focused on building an audit management system using Python, FastAPI, and Azure. This gave me the opportunity to contribute to a real software system that supports audit workflows, automation, and data processing.

Information about the Employer

Goals

Goal: I wanted to strengthen my problem-solving skills by working on software development and technically complex projects during my Cloud Engineering internship. My goal was to approach real-world technical challenges using code, cloud tools, and software systems, and to develop the ability to analyze problems, design effective solutions, and implement them efficiently.

Reflection: I made strong progress toward this goal while working on the audit management system. Building features with Python, FastAPI, and Azure required me to break down complex business needs into smaller technical problems. I worked on automating email reminders, identifying late audits, and supporting ETL pipelines, which helped me become more confident in analyzing requirements, testing solutions, and improving workflows through code.

Goal: I wanted to improve my technological literacy by deepening my understanding of cloud platforms, software systems, and modern development tools used in real-world environments. My goal was to move beyond surface-level knowledge and develop a stronger conceptual and practical understanding of how cloud infrastructure, applications, and services interact.

Reflection: This term helped me better understand how backend applications, cloud services, databases, and automated workflows connect in a professional environment. Working with Azure and FastAPI showed me how cloud-based applications are structured, deployed, and supported. I also gained more confidence working with unfamiliar tools and thinking about technology choices in terms of scalability, reliability, security, and maintainability.

Goal: I wanted to have the opportunity to work on a large-scale project that became my primary focus during the internship and involved significant software development and technical decision-making. My goal was to contribute meaningfully to a complex project from planning to implementation, rather than only working on small isolated tasks.

Reflection: I was able to meet this goal through my work on the audit management system. This project became a major focus of my term and allowed me to contribute to backend development, automation, data processing, and cloud-related work. Learning the system's architecture and understanding how my work fit into the larger audit process helped me feel more ownership over my contributions and improved my ability to work within a complex software project.

Overall, my fourth co-op term at Co-operators was a valuable experience that helped me grow as a software developer and cloud engineering student. Over the four-month term, I worked on a meaningful internal system and applied my technical skills to real business problems involving audits, reminders, late audit tracking, and ETL pipelines.

This term also helped me understand the importance of building maintainable systems that support business users and reduce manual effort. I learned how important it is to write reliable backend logic, think carefully about data movement, and communicate clearly with the people guiding and reviewing the work.

I am grateful for the experience I gained during this term, and I am excited to continue with Co-operators for another four months. Continuing in the role will give me the chance to build on the foundation I have developed and contribute even more deeply to the audit management system.

In my role as a Cloud Engineering Co-op at Co-operators, I worked on building an audit management system using Python, FastAPI, and Azure. The system supports audit-related workflows by helping automate communication, track late audits, and process data through ETL pipelines.

A major part of my work involved developing automation for email reminders and creating logic to look up audits that were late or required follow-up. I also contributed to ETL workflows that helped move, transform, and organize data so it could be used more effectively within the audit management system.

Conclusions

Overall, my time at Co-operators was an excellent learning experience. It gave me the opportunity to apply software development and cloud engineering skills in a real business environment while contributing to a system that improved audit workflows. Working with Python, FastAPI, Azure, automation, and ETL pipelines helped me strengthen both my technical ability and my understanding of how cloud-based systems support organizational processes.

I would like to express my gratitude to Aika Palad for guiding me throughout my co-op term at Co-operators. Her support, feedback, and mentorship helped me improve my technical skills, understand the project more clearly, and grow professionally during my time with the team.
